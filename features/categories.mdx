## How to Use Categories

### Manual Transaction Entry

When registering a transaction manually, select the appropriate category from the dropdown menu:

1. Navigate to **Registro** page
2. Choose transaction type (Gasto or Ingreso)
3. Select the category that best matches your transaction
4. Complete the remaining fields and submit

<Note>
The category dropdown automatically updates based on whether you're registering an expense or income.
</Note>

### AI Chat Assistant

When using the AI chat to register transactions, simply mention the category in natural language:

<Accordion title="Example prompts">
  - "Register a $50 expense for Alimentación"
  - "Add income of $1500 from Tours with 20 passengers"
  - "Record $200 Transporte expense for gasoline"
</Accordion>

The AI will automatically recognize and apply the correct category.

### Excel/CSV Import

When importing transactions from Excel, include the category name in the `categoria` column:

```csv
fecha,tipo,categoria,monto,descripcion
2025-10-06,gasto,Alimentación,350,Restaurant dinner
2025-10-06,ingreso,Tours,1200,Morning group tour
```

<Warning>
Make sure category names match exactly (including accents) to ensure proper classification.
</Warning>

### Receipt Scanning

When you upload a receipt photo, the AI automatically suggests a category based on the receipt content. You can accept the suggestion or change it before saving.

## Viewing by Category

You can analyze your spending and income patterns by category:

- **Dashboard**: View category breakdowns in the analytics section
- **Reports**: Generate detailed reports grouped by category
- **Data Views**: Sort and filter transactions by category to see specific spending patterns

<Tip>
Regularly reviewing your category breakdown helps identify spending trends and opportunities for savings.
</Tip>
